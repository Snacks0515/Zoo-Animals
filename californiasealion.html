Physical Profile

Range in Size: Males can grow to around 6.5 to 8 feet long and weigh 400 to 850 pounds or more. While the Females can grow to 5 to 6.5 feet and weigh up to 110 - 250 pounds.

Identification Markings
  • Ear Flaps
  • Long narrow snouts
  • A forehead bump would also be a sign that one is a male
  • Males have dark brown fur
  • Female have lighter tan or blonde fur

Habitat and Geography

Habitats
  • Shallow coastal waters
  • Sandy/rocky shores of the Eastern North Pacific Ocean
Distribution
  • Northern Limit: Southeast Alaska and British Columbia, Canada
  • Southern Limit: Central Mexico, including the Gulf of California and the Pacific coast of Baja California

    • Distribution Map

    Ecology and Behavior

    Feeding and Diet: Anchovies, mackerel, sardines, rockfish, pacific whiting, and salmon

    Behaviors: Commonly form large noisy groups

    Adaptations: Rotating hind flippers that allow for faster movement on land, blubber insulation, long sensitive whiskers that detect underwater movements and vibrations, tightly close their ear flaps and nostrils to keep water out

    Communication: Males will bark like dogs do to show or claim dominace

    Life History Cycle:
    1. Years 0-1: Born on land and weigh about 15 pounds
    2. Years 1-5: Sea lion reaches maturity
    3. Years 5+: Males don't reach their full size until years 9-12
    4. Females also tend to live 5 years longer than males who usually only live for around 15-20 years

    Breeding Behaviors:

    Predators: Orcas, and large sharks like the Great White Shark
