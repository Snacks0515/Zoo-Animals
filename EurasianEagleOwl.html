Physical Profile

Identification Markings
  • prominent feathery ear tufts
  • Large
  • Deep orange to pumpkin-colored eyes
  • Pale buffy underparts with fine streaking
  • Facial disk less pronounced than some other owls, edged with darker feathers

Ecology and Behavior

Feeding and Diet: The Eurasion Eagle Owl has a wide variety of prey that it can feed on such as rabbits, rodents, foxes), birds, reptiles, amphibians, and large insects

Behaviors and Adaptations: Excellent night vision, acute hearing, powerful flight to ambush prey, solitary outside of breeding season, can fly silently due to specialized feather structure, and has adapted to cold climates with dense plumage.

Communication: Vocalizations include deep, resonant hoots used for territory defense and mating calls, and also uses visual displays.

Life History Cycle & Breeding Behaviors: Nests on cliff ledges, rocky outcrops, or sometimes in abandoned nests of other large birds.

Predators: Due to their size and strength the only actual big predators are humans.
