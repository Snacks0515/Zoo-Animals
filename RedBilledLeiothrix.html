Physical Profile

Range in Size:

  • Length: Approximately 6 inches
  • Weight: Around 20-25 grams
Identification Markings
  • Bright red bill
  • Golden-yellow throat and breast with orange and red hues
  • Olive-green upperparts and yellow wings with red patches
  • Tail feathers have a unique pattern with yellow edges
  • Overall plump and colorful appearance

Habitat and Geography

Habitats
  • Dense forest understory, bamboo thickets, and shrubbery
  • Found in subtropical or tropical moist lowland and montane forests
    Distribution
  • Native to southern China, the Himalayas, and parts of Southeast Asia
  • Some populations exist in Hawaii, Japan, and some parts of Europe due to escape or release from captivity

Distribution Map

Ecology and Behavior

Feeding and Diet: Fruits, berries and insects

Behaviors: Highly social and agile

Adaptations: Adapted to forest understory life with strong legs for hopping and climbing

Communication: Variety of musical calls and melodious whistles

Life History Cycle & Breeding Behaviors: Breeding generally occurs in spring and summer, both parents feed and care for the young, juveniles fledge after about 2-3 weeks.

Predators: Snakes, larger birds, and small mammals
