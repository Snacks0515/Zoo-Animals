Magnificent Tree Frog

Common Name: Magnificent Tree Frog (also known as Splendid Tree Frog)Status/Conservation: Not threatened - safe

Classification
kingdom Animalia
Phylum Chordata
Subphylum Vertebrata
Class Amphibia
Subclass Lissamphibia
Order Anura
Suborder Neobatrachia
Family Pelodryadidae
Genus Pelodryas
Species Pelodryas splendida

Physical Profile

Range in Size: Max growth size is 4-4.7 inches

Identification Markings
  • Bright green back
  • White or yellow spots scattered on the back
  • White belly
  • A poison sac that is located on the back of the head

Habitat and Geography

Habitats
  • Wet, forested environments such as rainforests and woodlands
  • Bodies of water like streams, ponds, and swamps
    Distribution
  • Native to Australia, primarily in the northern and eastern regions
  • Found in tropical and subtropical zones

Distribution Map

Ecology and Behavior

Feeding and Diet: Mainly eats insects and small vertebrate

Behaviors: Dwells in trees but also time near water

Adaptations: Poison gland for defense against predators

Communication: Males usually use mating calls and other vocalizations for dominance

Life History Cycle & Breeding Behaviors: Lays eggs in water during wet periods and usually live for about 8 years

Predators: Birds, snakes, and reptiles and mammals that are bigger than them
